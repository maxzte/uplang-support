Privacy Policy for UpLang

Last updated: November 24, 2026

UpLang ("we", "our", or "the extension") is a Chrome browser extension designed to help users practice foreign language listening comprehension on YouTube Music™.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, or payment details.
  • We do not track your browsing history or monitor activity outside of music.youtube.com.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or behavioral tracking scripts.
  • We do not sell or monetize user data in any manner.

2. Storage & Data Persistence

Local Data (chrome.storage.local): Your SRS flashcards, SM-2 review progress, custom lyrics timing offsets, learning streak, and practice statistics are stored locally on your device.

Synchronized Preferences (chrome.storage.sync): If Chrome Sync is enabled, your extension settings (such as study mode, challenge frequency, font size, and translation language) are synced via your Google account using Chrome Storage Sync APIs. No personal identifiers or listening histories are stored in sync storage.

3. Network Requests and Third-Party Services

  • YouTube / YouTube Music (music.youtube.com, www.youtube.com, *.googlevideo.com): Retrieves player track metadata and official timedtext captions/subtitles.
  • LRCLIB (lrclib.net): Queries open-source synchronized song lyrics using track artist, title, and duration.
  • Free Dictionary APIs (freedictionaryapi.com, api.dictionaryapi.dev): Queried only when you click a word to retrieve definitions and phonetics.

4. Microphone & Voice Recognition

Voice input is optional and activated only when you explicitly click the microphone button. Speech is processed via your browser's Web Speech API; UpLang does not record or transmit raw audio to any UpLang server.

5. On-Device AI

Contextual word explanations and line translations use Chrome's built-in on-device AI (LanguageModel and Translator APIs). All AI inference runs locally on your device.

6. Contact

Questions or feedback? Open an issue at github.com/lozovskyi/upeng/issues.
